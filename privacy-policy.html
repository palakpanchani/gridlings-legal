Privacy Policy

How the Gridlings Android game handles information.

Applies to: Gridlings for Android (package com.gridlings.puzzle), version 1.0.0
Effective date: 23 October 2026

Draft for review. This policy has been prepared from the app's current build and has not been legally reviewed or approved. Replace every highlighted placeholder and remove this notice before publishing.

In short

  • Gridlings has no accounts, sign-in, in-app purchases, analytics or crash-reporting service of our own.
  • Your progress, coins, player name and settings are stored only on your device. We don't run our own servers for Gridlings and don't receive them.
  • The game uses three Google services that connect to the internet: Google AdMob for optional rewarded video ads, Firebase Cloud Messaging for game announcements, and Firebase Remote Config for game and ad settings. These services receive some device information, described below.
  • Ads only play when you tap a button to watch one. Gridlings asks Google to show non-personalised ads with teen protections to every player.

1. Who we are

Gridlings is developed and published by Puzzle Games Master (“we”, “us”).

For the information the game itself keeps on your device, and for our choices about which services the game uses, we are responsible for how this policy is applied. Google is responsible for its own processing as described in Google's policies, linked below.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following in its private storage on your phone or tablet so you can continue where you left off:

  • Puzzle progress: completed levels, stars, best times and scores, XP and achievements.
  • In-game items: coins, lives, hints, collection pieces, reward chests and your chosen companion.
  • Daily Challenge days and streak.
  • The player name you choose (up to 16 characters). You don't have to use your real name.
  • Your settings: Sound, Music, Vibration and Notifications.
  • Housekeeping for features, such as when the last reminder was sent, how many reward videos you've watched today, and the last game and ad settings downloaded from Firebase Remote Config.

The game does not send this information to us or to anyone else. Gridlings is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so this information stays on the device where you play. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

4. Advertising and rewarded ads

Gridlings shows only rewarded video ads, and only when you choose to watch one. Each ad starts from a button that names the reward. You can always decline and keep playing. The game has no banner, interstitial or app-open ads.

You can currently choose to watch an ad for:

  • an extra life when you've run out of lives;
  • coins on the Rewards screen or Coin Shop;
  • a hint when you have no free hints and not enough coins;
  • a retry of a level when you have no lives left.

Each type of reward has a daily limit, and rewards are given only after the ad reports that you've earned them.

How ads are configured

  • Before any ad is requested, Gridlings asks Google to apply teen ad treatment to every player. Google describes this as turning off personalised ads and remarketing and applying its teen ad-serving protections.
  • Ad content is limited to Google's G (general audiences) rating.
  • Ads are only loaded once Google's consent check says ads may be requested (see Consent and privacy choices).

Even when ads are not personalised, Google may use device information and identifiers to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ads, and prevent fraud and abuse.

The game never shares your progress, coins or player name with Google or advertisers.

5. Firebase services

Firebase Cloud Messaging lets us send occasional game announcements. To deliver them, Firebase gives this installation of the game a Firebase installation ID and a messaging token. These identify the app installation, not you by name. Gridlings does not send your progress or player name to Firebase, and we don't operate our own server that stores the token. Switching off Notifications in the game's Settings deletes the messaging token on your device, and announcements stop.

Firebase Remote Config lets us adjust some settings without an app update, such as whether reward videos are on, how many coins a reward video gives, and daily limits. The game downloads these settings from Firebase and keeps a copy on your device. If you're offline, the game uses its last saved settings or built-in defaults.

6. Notifications

  • Reminders for the Daily Challenge, streaks and rewards are created on your device without using the internet. You'll get at most one a day, and none once you've played that day's challenge.
  • Announcements are delivered through Firebase Cloud Messaging (see above).

You can turn notifications off at any time in Settings › Notifications in the game, or in your device's notification settings. On Android 13 and later, Android asks for your permission first.

7. How information is used and shared

Information is used to:

  • save your game on your device and run its features;
  • show rewarded ads you choose to watch, and give you the reward;
  • ask for and remember consent for ads where the law requires it;
  • deliver reminders and announcements you haven't switched off;
  • adjust game and ad settings remotely.

We don't sell your personal information. The only parties that receive information from the game are the Google services listed above. We may disclose information if required by law, but in practice we don't hold player data on our own systems.

Google may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States, under the safeguards described in its policies.

Where data protection laws such as the UK GDPR or EU GDPR apply, the legal basis for processing is your consent for advertising where consent is required (collected through Google's consent message), and our legitimate interests in running the game, delivering announcements you haven't switched off, and adjusting game settings.

8. Consent and privacy choices

  • Ad consent. Each time the game starts, it checks with Google's User Messaging Platform whether consent is needed where you live (for example in the UK, EEA or Switzerland). If it is, Google's consent message is shown. Ads are only requested when Google reports that they may be.
  • Changing your ad choices. When required in your region, a Privacy options entry appears in the game's Settings so you can review or change your choices.
  • You never have to watch ads. Lives refill over time, and coins and hints can be earned by playing.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings (on most devices: Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads).
  • Notifications. Turn them off in the game's Settings or in your device settings.

10. Retention, security and deletion

  • On your device: game data stays until you uninstall the game or clear its storage (Android Settings › Apps › Gridlings › Storage › Clear storage). Either deletes all progress permanently, and it can't be restored because there is no cloud copy.
  • Messaging token: deleted from your device when you switch off Notifications in the game.
  • Information held by Google: kept and deleted according to Google's retention policies. See How Google retains data and Firebase privacy.

Game data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps can't normally read. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we keep data collection to what the game needs.

Because we don't keep player data on our own systems, we usually can't find or delete information about a particular player. If you have a question or request, contact us and we'll help where we can, including pointing you to Google's tools.

11. Children's privacy

Gridlings is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. The game doesn't ask for age, and it applies the same teen ad protections and G-rated ad content to every player.

We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game and you have a concern, please contact us.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information, such as to access it, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its use, port it, or withdraw consent. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect processing that already happened.

Most game data never leaves your device, so you can delete it yourself as described above. For information held by Google, you can use Google's tools or contact Google. You can also contact us using the details below.

If you are in the UK, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). In the EEA, you can contact your local data protection authority.

13. Changes to this policy

We'll update this policy if the game's features or the services it uses change, for example if cloud saves or new services are added. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed. For significant changes, we'll also let players know in the game or on its store page.
