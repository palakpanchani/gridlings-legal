About Gridlings

A casual logic puzzle game for Android. Match. Think. Discover.

Version 1.0.0

What is Gridlings?

Gridlings is a logic puzzle where the pieces have personality. Place the Gridlings on the board so that no row, column or region has the same one twice. Every puzzle can be solved by careful reasoning, with no guessing.

Start with small boards to learn the rule, then work your way up to full 9×9 puzzles that need real deduction.

Three worlds, 109 levels

Meadow

Gentle 4×4 boards to learn the rules.

Forest

6×6 boards that ask you to look a little closer.

Crystal Realm

Full 9×9 boards for experienced puzzlers.

How you play

  • Notes: pencil in the Gridlings a cell could be.
  • Hints: three free hints to start, then more with coins.
  • Hearts and lives: three hearts per puzzle, so every move counts. Lives refill over time.
  • Stars and best times: earn up to three stars on every level and replay to improve.
  • Daily Challenge: a new puzzle every day, with a streak reward on day seven.
  • Collect and celebrate: find all nine Gridlings (Ember, Moss, Aqua, Luna, Spark, Bloom, Cloud, Crystal and Nova), choose a companion, open reward chests and unlock achievements.
  • Optional reward videos: watch one only if you want an extra life, coins, a hint or a retry. They're never required.

Gridlings plays offline, needs no account, and has no in-app purchases. Your progress stays on your device.
